Simple controls · Predictable results

Learn it by ear.

Each control always changes the same part of Icy Beauty. This guide describes what you will hear, where the useful ranges sit, and which controls work well together.

Start here

Hold a four-note chord and move one parameter at a time. Return it to 50% before trying the next control; Release defaults to 65%.

Colour

Tone

Tone changes both brightness and the balance of the three tone layers. The rounded main note stays present; raising Tone adds the octave, then brings the high interval forward and reduces smoothing.

  • Low: soft basses, rounded leads, and woody chords.
  • Middle: the balanced factory sound.
  • High: bright bells and glass-like leads.

Pair it with Resonance: Tone supplies harmonic detail and Resonance turns that detail into a clearer ring.

Slow movement

Motion

Every active voice receives its own slow pitch movement. Voices do not move together, so a held chord gently spreads and closes. This is pitch movement, not a chorus or delay effect.

0%No slow pitch movement
25%Very gentle drift
50%About ±6.5 cents around 0.54 Hz
75%Clearly animated ensemble movement
100%About ±13 cents around 1 Hz

The modulation wheel raises Motion toward maximum. Aftertouch adds a smaller amount.

Fast texture

Grain

Grain combines tiny random pitch changes with a noise layer. The first half stays subtle; the second half moves more quickly toward rough, weathered sounds. At 100%, the random pitch part is about ±2.6 cents.

  • Motion is slow and smooth.
  • Grain is fast and uneven.

Low Tone plus Grain sounds breathy and worn. High Tone plus Grain makes the bright layers feel brittle.

Tuned ring

Resonance

Resonance mixes in a pitched ring that follows every played note. It stays connected to the note rather than running away into self-oscillation.

  • Below 30%: a small outline around the note.
  • Around 50%: the factory sound’s clear icy edge.
  • Above 70%: an obvious glass-like ring.

Polyphonic aftertouch raises Resonance on one note. Channel pressure raises it across the chord.

Fade time

Release

Release sets how long a note fades after key-up or gate-low. The lower half gives precise short and medium values; the upper half opens into long tails.

0%0.08 s
25%0.83 s
50%3.08 s
65%5.15 s
75%6.83 s
100%12.08 s

Higher Resonance usually makes a long Release easier to hear.

Polyphony

Voices

Choose one to eight voices when adding the algorithm. MIDI and CV share this pool. Output gain is adjusted for the count so adding voices does not cause a large level jump.

1Bass, melody, or external arpeggiation
2Intervals and duophonic lines
4The balanced default
6Extended chords
8Full polyphony and dense MIDI playing

Global transpose

Pitch offsets

The Setup page’s Semitones and Octaves controls transpose every MIDI and CV voice. Semitones covers −11 to +11; Octaves covers −4 to +4. Both default to zero and update held notes immediately.

Sample & hold continues to hold the sampled input CV while either global pitch offset can still be changed.
